# Shineovative WhatsApp CRM — Recommended Business Defaults

> **Purpose:** Recommended business default configuration for Shineovative Solutions internal CRM usage. Review and approve before seeding production database defaults.

---

## 1. Sales Pipeline & Stages (Deals)

Recommended single primary sales pipeline: **"Shineovative Sales Pipeline"**

| Position | Stage Name | Color Hex | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **New Lead** | `#00b4ff` (Electric Blue) | Inbound contact or lead inquiry from website/WhatsApp |
| 2 | **Qualified / Contacted** | `#3b82f6` (Blue) | Initial discovery call or chat qualification complete |
| 3 | **Proposal Sent** | `#f59e0b` (Amber) | Formal project quote, demo, or proposal submitted |
| 4 | **Negotiation** | `#a855f7` (Purple) | Scope refinement, contract terms, or pricing discussion |
| 5 | **Closed Won** | `#22c55e` (Green) | Client signed agreement and paid initial deposit |
| 6 | **Closed Lost** | `#ef4444` (Red) | Deal closed without conversion |

## 2. Default Contact Tags

Recommended default tags:
* `Hot Lead` (`#00b4ff`) — High-intent lead requesting immediate callback/demo.
* `Existing Customer` (`#22c55e`) — Active Shineovative client with ongoing services.
* `Enterprise` (`#a855f7`) — High-value corporate or institutional lead.
* `Follow-up Needed` (`#f59e0b`) — Requires scheduled follow-up outreach.
* `VIP` (`#ec4899`) — High-priority strategic account.
